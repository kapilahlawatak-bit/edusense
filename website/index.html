How it works

1. Verified content

Every maths answer is re-derived by the engine before it enters the bank. Across thousands of items, zero wrong keys have shipped.

2. Tagged distractors

Each wrong option is labelled with the specific mistake it represents — a sign error, an off-by-one, a reversed generation.

3. Actionable feedback

Students get a diagnosis and a fix. Teachers get a "who needs me this week" list with a re-teaching script for each.
